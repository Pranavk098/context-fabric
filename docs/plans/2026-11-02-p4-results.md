### Re-run with an imperfect handoff

Rule written and committed **before** the run: `108c6e4`, `docs/plans/2026-11-02-restart-rerun-rule.md` (bars commit
`b6f1c26`). Handoff recall 46.9% (carry v2 measured); what it misses is re-gathered at the cache-write price and joins the
restarted context. Pessimistic case: 33.1% (carry v1). Bar unchanged: >= 25% of 40+ turn sessions, positive headroom.
Run by `scripts/restart_breakeven.py --recall` (commit `2bf703d`); modelled, one machine, n = 44.

| handoff recall | sessions with positive headroom | median headroom / session | median share of cost-to-finish | bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| complete (ceiling) | 18 of 44 = 40.9% | $1.57 | about 9% | not the counted figure |
| **46.9% (counts)** | **14 of 44 = 31.8%** | $0.89 | 4.1% | cleared |
| 33.1% (pessimistic) | 14 of 44 = 31.8% | $0.40 | 2.0% | cleared |

How thin is the pass? Counting headroom at each session's first positive turn: at 46.9% recall, 1 of the 14 is under
$0.10 (13 of 44 = 29.5% without it, still over the bar). At 33.1%, **4 of the 14 are under $0.10**; without them the share
is 10 of 44 = 22.7%, under the bar. So gate 1 holds at 46.9% but is fragile at 33.1%.

Read it honestly: the count survives (31.8% >= 25%), so gate 1 of 2 for P7 is met on this machine. But the headroom is
small and halves again in the pessimistic case, the sample is 44 sessions from one developer, and recall of edited files is
used as a proxy for all gathered context. The registered v2 benchmark (ADR-0013) found forced restarts costlier. Nothing in
the plugin restarts sessions. No saving claim.

## Method and caveats

- Restart at turn t pays the session's own first-turn prompt as a cache write plus the packet, then holds prefix + packet and re-grows by the recorded per-turn increments. Continuing pays the logged context on every remaining turn.
- The packet is assumed complete. A packet that misses part of the working set raises the restarted side and lowers every share here; if break-even needs that assumption to hold, it does not count (P4 M3).
- A pass after a failure in the same session is the only test boundary; a run that never failed first is not one. Task switches come from a fixed phrase list, so they are a proxy, not a semantic read.
- One person's sessions on one machine. Dollar figures use one price row per session (its most common model family); an unpriced family drops the session.
